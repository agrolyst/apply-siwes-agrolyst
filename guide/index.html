1. About the Agrolyst SIWES application portal

The Agrolyst SIWES Application Portal is the online starting point for students who want to apply for an industrial training opportunity with Agrolyst Integrated Farms and Services.

SIWES means Student Industrial Work Experience Scheme. It is intended to help eligible students gain practical exposure related to their education and career development. Agrolyst operates in agribusiness and integrated farm services, so the training area available to an applicant depends on programme needs, supervision capacity, eligibility and the decision made during application review.

This guide explains the normal online workflow and where to go for each task. Read the page carefully before submitting information. The online form, status page and recovery page are separate tools that work together:

Apply for SIWES

Use the public form to provide personal, academic, training and contact details. Open the application form.

Check application status

Use the Application ID and the email address used during application to view the latest available status. Open status lookup.

Recover an Application ID

If you lose the reference, request an email verification code. Phone recovery is temporarily disabled. Open ID recovery.

Applicant sign-in

Registered applicants can use the private portal when they need access to account-linked features. Open applicant sign-in.

Important: Creating an account or submitting an application does not guarantee placement. All applications and supporting information are subject to review by Agrolyst.
Preparation

2. Before you apply

Prepare your details before opening the form. This reduces errors and helps you finish the application without losing track of information.

  • Use your correct legal name, as it appears on your school records.
  • Use an email address you can open now. Application updates and recovery verification depend on access to the contact information associated with the application.
  • Have your current phone number, including the country code, ready.
  • Confirm the correct name of your institution, department, course and current level.
  • Know the proposed start and end dates requested by your school and the training duration you need.
  • Prepare a full contact address and optional emergency contact details.
  • Keep the required school introduction letter and student identification card ready if the application flow asks you to upload them.
  • Use a private device where possible, and do not share one-time verification codes with other people.
Use one application per applicant. Do not submit repeated applications to correct a spelling error or because you cannot find an earlier Application ID. First check your status or recover your existing ID. The service is intended to prevent duplicate applications from the same email address; if you think a duplicate was created, contact Agrolyst for assistance.
Read first

3. Applicant Policy and Privacy Notice

The first visit to the public application form presents the Applicant Policy and Privacy Notice. Read the complete policy before accepting it. The acknowledgement records that you have reviewed the rules and understand how the information you submit will be used for application administration.

The policy is there to protect both applicants and the programme. It covers the accuracy of submitted information, handling of documents, review and placement expectations, communication, privacy, and the responsible use of the portal.

What applicants should understand

  • Provide truthful, complete and up-to-date information.
  • Submit only documents that belong to you and that you are authorised to share.
  • Application submission is not the same as acceptance or placement.
  • Agrolyst may review details and request clarification or additional information.
  • Keep your Application ID and verification codes private.
  • Use the official portal links for application status and recovery.

If you need to read the notice again, use the Applicant Policy & Privacy Notice link on the application page. If you do not agree with the policy, do not continue with the application.

The portal may remember policy acceptance on the same browser for the current policy version. If the policy is updated, you may be asked to read and accept the updated version again.
Application walkthrough

4. How to apply for SIWES with Agrolyst

The public form uses a step-by-step process. Complete each section, use Save & Next to move forward, then review your answers before final submission.

01

Personal information

Enter your full name, accessible email address and telephone number. Check the email carefully because it is used to identify your application for status and recovery.

02

Academic background

Choose or type your institution, then provide your department, course of study and current level. If your institution is not listed in the suggestions, enter its official name carefully.

03

Training preferences

Select your preferred training area, required duration and proposed start and end dates. Make sure the end date is not earlier than the start date, and use dates that agree with your school’s requirements.

04

Contact and emergency details

Enter a usable contact address. Google address suggestions may appear while typing when the service is configured; if they do not appear, enter the address manually. Emergency contact details may be optional as indicated on the form.

05

Review and submit

Read the summary and use the Edit links if you need to change a section. Confirm that the email is accessible, acknowledge the privacy notice and submit only when the information is accurate.

What does Save & Next do?

Save & Next validates the visible section and saves a draft in the browser on the device you are using. A browser draft is not a submitted application and does not have an Application ID. If you change devices, clear browser data or use a private browsing window, a locally saved draft may not be available. Final submission is the step that sends your application to the application service.

Supporting documents

6. Required documents

Where the application or later compliance process requests supporting documents, use clear, readable files. Follow the instructions on the official portal for allowed file types and upload limits.

Core SIWES documents

  1. School Letter of Introduction: the official letter from your institution introducing you for industrial training.
  2. Valid Student Identification Card: a current student ID that clearly identifies you and your institution.

Optional supporting documents

  • Curriculum Vitae (CV), if requested or available.
  • Recent passport photograph, if requested by the form or compliance stage.

Additional information may include an emergency contact name and telephone number. Upload only genuine documents. Do not upload another student’s ID, an altered school letter or a file that contains unrelated private information.

Do not email documents to unofficial addresses. Upload sensitive records only through the official portal or provide them to authorised Agrolyst staff through a confirmed process.
Final submission

7. Review, confirmation and submission

Before the final submission, the portal asks you to confirm that your email address is accessible and acknowledge the privacy notice. These acknowledgements are part of the submission process.

After the form is submitted successfully, the service should return a confirmation with your Application ID. The confirmation should state that the application has been received and is Under Review. Save the ID immediately; you may copy it or write it down in a secure place.

If an error appears

  • Read the message and correct the field it identifies.
  • Check your internet connection and try again if the service reports a temporary problem.
  • Do not assume an application was submitted if the page did not show a successful confirmation and Application ID.
  • If you are unsure whether submission succeeded, use the status page if you have an ID or contact Agrolyst before submitting again.
A successful browser transition is not proof of submission on its own. Keep the actual confirmation and Application ID as your reference.
Keep this safe

8. What is an Application ID?

An Application ID is the unique reference associated with a submitted SIWES application. It helps the portal locate the correct record when you check status or contact support. It is not your password and it is not proof of acceptance by itself.

Store the ID privately. Avoid posting screenshots that reveal it in public groups or social media. If you have more than one submitted application, confirm that you are checking the correct reference.

How to use your Application ID

  1. Open the Check Application Status page.
  2. Enter your Application ID exactly as shown, including its letters and numbers.
  3. Enter the same email address used on the application.
  4. Submit the lookup form to view the latest available status.

The status page requires both the reference and matching email to reduce the risk of another person looking up your application using a guessed or shared ID.

Lost reference

10. How to recover a lost Application ID

If you submitted an application but misplaced the reference, open Recover Application ID. Recovery uses email verification to protect your application information. Phone-number recovery is temporarily disabled.

  1. Open the recovery page.
  2. Enter the email address you used for the original application.
  3. Request a one-time verification code.
  4. Check your inbox and spam or junk folder.
  5. Enter the code on the recovery page before it expires.
  6. After successful verification, review the application reference or references shown.

The recovery service may use a neutral message that does not reveal whether an email address has a matching application. This protects applicants from unwanted account and application discovery.

If the code does not arrive

  • Check that you typed the email address correctly.
  • Look in spam, junk, promotions and other filtered folders.
  • Wait briefly before requesting another code; repeated requests may be rate-limited.
  • Use the newest code received, as older codes may no longer work.
  • If you no longer control the email account, contact Agrolyst through an official channel for identity verification. Do not send passwords or verification codes to a support contact.
For now, choose email recovery only. The phone/SMS recovery option has been disabled temporarily.
Assessment

11. What happens while your application is under review?

After submission, the information provided may be assessed for completeness, eligibility, training needs, document validity and the programme’s capacity. The team may need to verify information or request additional details before making a decision.

Keep your email and telephone number active, check the status page from time to time, and respond to official requests within the time stated in the message. If your school changes your required dates, seek guidance rather than creating a second application.

Possible review outcomes

  • Continue waiting: the application is still under review.
  • Changes requested: the review team needs an update or clarification.
  • Approved: a decision has been recorded and further instructions may follow.
  • Not approved: the application was not approved.
  • Acceptance letter issued: an official letter has been generated and issued by Agrolyst.

Do not treat an informal message, an account registration, a submitted form or a status screenshot as an acceptance letter. An acceptance letter is valid only when it has been officially issued through the authorised process.

Official outcome

12. Acceptance letters and next steps

An acceptance letter is not available merely because you have completed the form. It becomes available only after the application has been approved and the letter has been officially issued. If the status page says the letter has been issued, sign in to the official applicant portal if your application is linked to your account and follow the portal’s instructions.

Read the letter carefully. Check your name, institution, training area, duration and dates. If any detail is incorrect, contact Agrolyst through an official channel before relying on the document. Do not edit the content of an official letter yourself.

After acceptance, follow the specific instructions communicated by Agrolyst and your institution. You may be asked to complete additional compliance steps, confirm resumption or provide other relevant records. Do not assume every applicant follows the same timeline.

Account access

13. Accounts, sign-in and linking an application

The public application flow is designed to let students begin without first signing in. The private applicant portal provides account-based features, such as viewing or completing application information when available to the account.

If the portal offers an application-linking process, use the same email address that belongs to the submitted application and follow the official verification instructions. Email matching alone should not be treated as sufficient proof if the portal asks for an additional verification code or staff-assisted identity check.

If you see “session expired” or “please log in again”

  • Open the official applicant sign-in page and sign in again.
  • Do not share your password or one-time code with anyone.
  • If you were completing a public application, remember that a local draft is different from an authenticated account session.
  • If the error continues after signing in again, take note of the page and the exact error message and contact portal support.

For account recovery, use the official sign-in page and its recovery options. The public Application ID recovery tool is separate from password reset and does not reset your account password.

Protect your details

14. Privacy and security tips for applicants

  • Use the official domain and check the address bar before entering private information.
  • Keep your Application ID, account password and one-time verification codes private.
  • Do not ask another applicant to submit your information under their email address.
  • Use a secure device and sign out after using a shared computer.
  • Check that uploaded files are readable and contain only information needed for the application.
  • Do not submit multiple applications to bypass a pending review.
  • Be cautious of anyone who promises guaranteed placement in exchange for money or asks for your password.
  • Contact Agrolyst through its official channels if you suspect that someone has used your details.

The portal uses submitted information to administer and review applications. Read the current Applicant Policy and Privacy Notice on the application page for the rules that apply to your submission.

Help and fixes

15. Troubleshooting common issues

The institution is not in the list

Start typing the official institution name and allow the suggestions to load. If it is still missing, enter the full official name manually. The suggestion list depends on the institutions currently stored in the portal’s database.

Google address suggestions do not appear

Check your internet connection and type at least three characters. If suggestions remain unavailable, enter your complete address manually. The address field should not prevent you from providing a valid address just because an external suggestion service is temporarily unavailable.

The Save & Next button does not continue

Check the required fields in the current section. Browser validation may identify an empty or incorrectly formatted value. Correct it and select Save & Next again.

I cannot find the Application ID

Check the confirmation shown after submission, then use the email recovery page. If you never received a successful submission confirmation, the application may not have been submitted.

The status page cannot find my application

Enter the Application ID and email used on the same application. Check for mistyped letters or digits and accidental spaces. If the problem continues, use the recovery page or contact Agrolyst.

I see a session-expired message

Sign in again to the private portal. If the message occurs on a public page, refresh the page and retry. Do not share authentication codes with anyone offering to fix the issue.

The recovery verification code has expired

Request a new code and use the newest message received. Codes may expire for security reasons. Phone recovery is currently disabled, so use the original application email.

Quick answers

16. Frequently asked questions

Do I need to create an account before applying?

The public application form is designed to allow an application to be started without signing in. Account-based features are handled through the private applicant portal.

Does applying guarantee a SIWES placement?

No. Every application is subject to review, and placement is not guaranteed.

How do I know that my application was submitted?

Look for the successful submission confirmation and Application ID. If the page shows an error and no ID, do not assume submission succeeded.

How do I check the status of my application?

Visit the status page, then enter your Application ID and the same email address used on the application.

Can I check status using only my email?

The public status lookup asks for both Application ID and application email. If you have lost your ID, use email verification on the recovery page.

Can I recover my Application ID by phone?

No. Phone-number recovery is temporarily disabled. Use email recovery instead.

What if my institution does not appear?

Enter the official institution name manually. The autocomplete list may not contain every institution yet.

Can I use the same email to apply again?

The service is intended to prevent duplicate submissions for the same email. If you need to correct an existing application, contact Agrolyst rather than creating another record.

When will my acceptance letter be available?

Only after approval and official issuance. Submission alone does not generate a valid acceptance letter.

What should I do if my phone number or email changes?

Use your current access to the official portal and contact Agrolyst for the approved process to update application contact details. Do not submit a duplicate just to change a contact.

Can I ask someone else to submit for me?

You are responsible for the accuracy of your details and for protecting your personal information. If you need assistance, use an authorised support process and verify any information entered on your behalf.

Does the status page show my uploaded documents?

No. The public status page is intended to show status information, not private documents or reviewer notes.

Need help?

17. Get assistance from Agrolyst

If you have read this guide and still need help, collect the relevant details before contacting the organisation. Explain which page you were using, what action you attempted, the exact error message, and whether you received an Application ID. Never include your password or one-time verification code in a support message.

If your question concerns an existing application, provide the Application ID only through an official, private contact channel when requested. If you lost the reference, try the recovery page first. If your issue concerns an acceptance letter, wait for the official status and follow the applicant portal instructions.
