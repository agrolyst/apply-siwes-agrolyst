How recovery works

  • Choose email or phone.
  • Enter the contact detail used on your application.
  • If it matches a submitted application, the Application ID is sent privately to you.
  • Open the status page and use your Application ID with your email to view updates.

Keep your details safe

Never share an Application ID, verification code or private application documents publicly. If you no longer have access to your original email or phone number, contact Agrolyst for identity verification.
